---
type: concept
title: Less reverse bias, more current
teaches:
  - B-004-004-006
  - B-004-004-008
exam_fact: Decreasing an FET's reverse bias increases its current. The gate never conducts current, so an FET is a high-impedance device.
---
In a junction FET the gate is **reverse biased**. More reverse bias pinches the channel narrower; **decrease the reverse bias** and the channel opens, so **current increases**.

Because the gate is reverse biased, **it never conducts current**. That's why an FET is a **high impedance** device: it barely loads the circuit driving it.
