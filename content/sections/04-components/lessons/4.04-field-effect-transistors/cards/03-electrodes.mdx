A **field-effect transistor (FET)** has a **gate**, a **drain** and a **source**.

- The **channel** runs between the **source and drain**, its two ends.
- Charge carriers **enter at the source** and **leave at the drain**.
- The **gate** is the **control electrode**: its voltage sets the **resistance of the channel**.
