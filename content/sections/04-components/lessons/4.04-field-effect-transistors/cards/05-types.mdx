exam_fact: FETs come in N channel and P channel. The FET source corresponds to the bipolar emitter, the drain to the collector (and the gate to the base).
---
The channel material gives the two types: **N channel** and **P channel**. (NPN and PNP are bipolar types.)

Each FET electrode has a bipolar twin:

| FET | Bipolar |
| --- | --- |
| Gate | Base |
| Source | **Emitter** |
| Drain | **Collector** |
