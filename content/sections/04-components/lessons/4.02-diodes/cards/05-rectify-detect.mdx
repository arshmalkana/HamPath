---
- Changing **AC to DC** is called **rectification**. Put AC on the **anode** and only the positive half-cycles reach the **cathode**: **pulsating direct current**, always the same polarity but not steady. A power supply smooths it afterwards.
- Diodes also recover the audio from a received AM signal, by the same one-way trick. That's called **detection**.
