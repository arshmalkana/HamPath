- A **Zener diode** is built to break down at a precise reverse voltage and hold it there. Its job is to **regulate**: keep a voltage **constant**.
- A **light-emitting diode (LED)** glows when forward biased. Its **chemical composition** sets the colour: red, green, blue or infrared. Fluorescent tubes and neon bulbs glow too, but they're gas discharge lamps, not semiconductors.
