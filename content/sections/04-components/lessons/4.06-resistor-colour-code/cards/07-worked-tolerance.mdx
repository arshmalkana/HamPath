**What are the possible values of a 100 Ω resistor with a 10% tolerance?**

1. 10% of 100 Ω = 10 Ω.
2. Lowest: 100 − 10 = **90 Ω**.
3. Highest: 100 + 10 = **110 Ω**.

Answer: **90 Ω to 110 Ω**. Tolerance works both ways.
