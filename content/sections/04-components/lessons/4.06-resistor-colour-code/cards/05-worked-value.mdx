**Red = 2, violet = 7, yellow = 4. What is the resistor's value?**

1. First two bands are digits: red, violet gives **27**.
2. The third band is the multiplier: yellow = 4, so add **four zeros**.
3. 27 followed by 0000 = **270 000 Ω = 270 kΩ**.

Watch the trap: 274 Ω reads the third band as a digit. It's a multiplier.
