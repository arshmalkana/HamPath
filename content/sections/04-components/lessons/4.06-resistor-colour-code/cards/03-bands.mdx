exam_fact: On a four-band resistor, bands 1 to 3 give the value in ohms (digit, digit, multiplier) and band 4 gives the tolerance in percent.
---
Read from the end where the bands are bunched together:

1. **First band**: first digit.
2. **Second band**: second digit.
3. **Third band**: the multiplier, the number of zeros to add.
4. **Fourth band**: the **tolerance** in percent.

So the **first three bands** give the **value in ohms**. The bands don't show the power rating or the material.

- **33 Ω and 39 Ω**: same first digit and multiplier. Only the **second** band differs.
- **120 Ω and 1200 Ω**: same digits, one more zero. Only the **third** band, the multiplier, differs.
