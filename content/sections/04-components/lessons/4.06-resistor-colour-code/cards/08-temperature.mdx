---
Resistance drifts with temperature, but which way depends on the resistor. The **temperature coefficient** tells you: a fraction of the value per **degree Celsius**. Positive and it rises as things warm; negative and it falls.

So "how does resistance change with rising temperature?" has one honest answer: **it depends on its temperature coefficient**.
