**Tolerance** says how far the real value may be from the marked one, either way.

A **100 Ω, 10%** resistor: 10% of 100 is 10, so it may measure anywhere from **90 Ω to 110 Ω**.

Smaller tolerance means higher **precision**. Of 0.1%, 5%, 10% and 20%, **0.1%** is the most precise and **20%** the least.
