---
- Like transistors, triodes **can amplify signals**. Only the tube has electrons crossing a vacuum and needs heat to make them move.
- A tube may be chosen because **it may handle higher power**. It's bigger, uses more current and needs higher voltages.
- The **field-effect transistor** behaves most like a triode: both are voltage controlled, with the gate playing the grid.
