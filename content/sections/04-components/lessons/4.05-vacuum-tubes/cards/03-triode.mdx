A **triode** is a tube with three electrodes: a **cathode**, a **single grid** and a **plate**, inside a glass envelope holding **a vacuum**. (A diode has two electrodes; tetrodes and pentodes add more grids.)

- The heated **cathode emits electrons**.
- The **plate** runs at the **highest positive voltage** and collects them.
- The **output current** flows between the **cathode and plate**.
- The **grid**, between them, is the **control element**: a little more negative and fewer electrons get through.
