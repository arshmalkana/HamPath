---
type: concept
title: NPN and PNP aren't interchangeable
teaches:
  - B-004-003-005
  - B-004-003-007
exam_fact: "You can't swap a PNP for an NPN: their polarities are reversed."
---
An **NPN** and a **PNP** transistor do the same job, but their **polarities are reversed**: an NPN wants its collector positive, a PNP wants it negative. Swap one for the other and every voltage in the circuit is the wrong way round. It isn't about gain, labels or frequency response.
