---
- **Saturation**: enough base current that the transistor is fully on. More base current changes nothing.
- **Cut-off**: no base current, so no collector current. Fully off.

Driven back and forth between the two, the transistor behaves like **a switch**, and the **base** decides which state it's in. Saturation and cut-off are normal operating states.

What really kills transistors is **excessive heat**. That's why power transistors sit on heat sinks.
