The **transistor** is the most basic semiconductor part that **amplifies**. A **bipolar transistor** can amplify a small signal using **low voltages**; small signal in, bigger copy out is **amplification**.

- Three electrodes: **collector, emitter and base**.
- A small current into the **base** controls the much larger current from collector to emitter. So the **base controls the output current**.
- Two types: **NPN** and **PNP**. "General purpose audio NPN device" on a label means a bipolar transistor.

(Gate, source and drain belong to field-effect transistors. Thyristors, SCRs and triacs are switching devices, not small-signal amplifiers.)
