title: Feedback and efficiency
teaches:

exam_fact: Excessive positive feedback makes an amplifier oscillate. Efficiency is output power divided by DC input power.
---
- **Positive feedback**: if some of the output finds its way back to the input in step with it, the amplifier keeps re-amplifying itself. Too much and **oscillations appear**: it becomes an oscillator, whistling on its own.
- **Efficiency** is the ratio of **output power to DC input power**. An amplifier drawing 100 W from the supply to deliver 50 W of RF is 50% efficient; the other 50 W becomes heat.
