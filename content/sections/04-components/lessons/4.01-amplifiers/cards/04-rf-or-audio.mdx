Pick the amplifier for the frequency it has to handle:

- Weak signals from the **antenna** are radio frequency, so use an **RF amplifier**.
- Weak signals from a **microphone** are audio, so use an **audio amplifier**.

The **speech amplifier** in a transceiver only needs to pass voice: about **300 Hz to 3 000 Hz**. That's enough to understand speech and keeps the transmitted signal narrow. Hi-fi's 40 Hz to 40 kHz would waste spectrum.
