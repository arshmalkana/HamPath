An **amplifier** is a circuit designed to **increase the amplitude** of a signal. Depending on the design it can boost the signal's **voltage**, current or power. It doesn't change the frequency or the phase on purpose.

- The increase is called **gain**. A part with gain has the property of **amplification**.
- Anything labelled **"Gain = 10 dB"** is an amplifier: 10 dB is ten times the power.
- Don't confuse it with an **attenuator** or audio fader (they make signals smaller), an **oscillator** (makes a signal from nothing) or a **modulator** (puts information onto a carrier).
