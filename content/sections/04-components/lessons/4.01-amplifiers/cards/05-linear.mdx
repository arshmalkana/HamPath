---
type: concept
title: Push too hard and it distorts
teaches:
  - B-004-001-002
exam_fact: If an amplifier becomes non-linear, its output becomes distorted.
---
A **linear** amplifier's output is an exact, bigger copy of its input. Push it past what its supply can deliver and the peaks get **clipped flat**: the amplifier has become **non-linear**, and the output is **distorted**.

On the air, distortion splatters onto nearby frequencies. Keep drive levels where the output stays clean.
