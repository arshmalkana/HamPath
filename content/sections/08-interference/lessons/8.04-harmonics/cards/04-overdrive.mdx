title: Overdriving makes harmonics
teaches:
  - B-008-004-009
  - B-008-004-011
exam_fact: Overdriven stages, or excessive drive to the power amplifier, cause excessive harmonics.
---
Push a stage too hard and it clips. Clipping creates **harmonics**. **Overdriven stages**, or **excessive drive** to the power amplifier, are a common cause.
