**Harmonic emissions** are unwanted signals at **multiples of the operating frequency**. They're unwanted because they **can interfere with other stations**.

- On 7.125 MHz but heard on 21.375 MHz: 3 × 7.125 = 21.375, your **third harmonic**.
- An interfering signal on **56 MHz**: the **second harmonic** of a **10 m** (28 MHz) transmission.
- TV trouble on **one or two channels only**, only when you're on **15 m**: harmonics. 3 × 21 MHz = 63 MHz is in channel 3.
