- **Splatter** is caused by **overmodulating** the transmitter.
- An SSB amplifier **flat-tops** (goes non-linear) from **excessive microphone gain**.
- Energy just **outside the necessary bandwidth** is an **out-of-band emission**. (Spurious emissions are further away.)
