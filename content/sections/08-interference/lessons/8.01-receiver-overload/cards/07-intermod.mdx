**Intermodulation** is when two or more strong signals **mix** to produce new frequencies, landing on the one you want.

- It can happen **outside** your receiver (two commercial transmitters mixing in a rusty joint) or **inside** it, when strong signals overload its front end.
- A handheld on an outside **gain antenna** suddenly hears **a mixture of signals with different modulation**: receiver intermodulation.
- Cure: a **suitable filter at the receiver** to keep the strong signals out.

A TV on channel 5 (76 to 82 MHz) hit only while you're on 14 MHz, near a 92.5 MHz FM station? 92.5 − 14 = 78.5 MHz: your signal mixes with the FM station in the TV. Start with a **high-pass filter at the TV**.
