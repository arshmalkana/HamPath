**Receiver overload** happens when a **very strong signal** swamps a receiver's front end. Your transmitter can be perfectly clean.

The tell-tale clue: the interference appears **whatever band you transmit on**. A harmonic would only hit certain channels.

The first fix goes on the **TV**: a **high-pass filter** that blocks your HF signal before it reaches the TV's input.
