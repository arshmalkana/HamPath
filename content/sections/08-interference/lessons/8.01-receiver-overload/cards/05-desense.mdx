A strong nearby signal can **desensitize** a receiver: its gain is pushed down and weak signals vanish, even though the strong signal isn't on the same frequency.

- Field Day: the 20 m **SSB station loses its signals** whenever the 20 m CW station transmits.
- Two **mobiles** working through a repeater have trouble once they're **close together**: each transmitter desensitizes the other's receiver.
