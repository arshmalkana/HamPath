**Audio rectification** happens when RF is picked up by audio wiring and detected by a non-linear part, in a device with **no RF components**.

- **SSB** comes out as **distorted speech**; **CW** as **on-and-off humming or clicking**.
- It's heard **across the whole dial** of a broadcast receiver, and **regardless of the volume setting**: muffled SSB in the living room even with the volume down.
