exam_fact: "Interference to a telephone: fit a modular plug-in RFI filter close to the telephone. With an off-centre-fed antenna causing audio interference in the shack, add a common-mode choke on the feedline."
---
- If a properly operating station causes **telephone** interference, install a **modular plug-in telephone RFI filter** right at the phone.
- With an **off-centre-fed** (unbalanced) antenna, current flows on the outside of the coax into the shack. Distorted audio from a nearby amplified speaker? Add a **common-mode choke** on the feedline.
