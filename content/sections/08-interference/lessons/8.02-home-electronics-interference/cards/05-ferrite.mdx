exam_fact: Coils on ferrite cores reduce HF interference to home electronics. Wrap speaker and audio leads around ferrite cores, and keep speaker leads short.
---
- Install **coils on ferrite cores**: wind the speaker, intercom or telephone leads **around a ferrite core**. It chokes off RF but passes audio.
- **Shorten the leads**. Long speaker wires make better antennas.
