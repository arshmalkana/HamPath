- **Spurious emissions**: signals your transmitter sends **outside the band** where it's transmitting.
- Operate a transmitter **without its cover or shielding** and it **may radiate** them.
- If people near your frequency hear interference from your **handheld**, it may be **transmitting spurious emissions**.
- **Intermodulation** is the undesired creation of **new frequencies** when signals enter a **non-linear** device.
