- **Local** clicks (in a nearby radio or TV) come from **making and breaking the circuit at the key**.
- Clicks heard **at a distance**, spreading over a wide bandwidth, come from **too-sharp rise and decay times** of the keyed carrier: **poor waveform shaping**.
- Fix: **check the keying filter and the stages after it**.
