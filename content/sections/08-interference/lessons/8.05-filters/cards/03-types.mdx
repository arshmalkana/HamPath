title: Five filter types
teaches:
  - B-008-005-001
  - B-008-005-002
  - B-008-005-003
  - B-008-005-011
exam_fact: Low-pass attenuates above its cut-off; high-pass attenuates below it. Band-pass attenuates above and below a range. A notch attenuates one frequency and passes all others.
---
| Filter | Passes | Blocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Low-pass** | below the cut-off | **above** it (e.g. above 40 MHz) |
| **High-pass** | above the cut-off | **below** it (e.g. below 60 MHz) |
| **Band-pass** | a range | **above and below** the range |
| **Band-reject** | everything else | a range |
| **Notch** | everything else | **one frequency** |
