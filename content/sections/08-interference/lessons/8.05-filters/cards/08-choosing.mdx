- A cheap **SDR** hearing stations from **much higher frequencies**: **low-pass**.
- An **AC line filter** passes 60 Hz and blocks RF: **low-pass**.
- A strong signal **very close** to yours: **notch**.
- **Field Day**, each receiver on its own band: **band-pass** on each.
- A **31 m broadcaster** hitting 40 m and 30 m: **band-reject** around it.
- **2 m** overloaded by nearby **land mobile** transmitters: **band-pass** for 2 m.
