exam_fact: A voltage drop is the loss of voltage caused by current flowing through part of a circuit. Polarity describes the direction of current in a DC circuit.
---
In the second lab, the two resistors shared the battery's 12 V between them. The voltage across each one is its **voltage drop**: voltage "used up" as current flows through it.

In a DC circuit current flows one way, from the battery's + terminal round to −. That direction is the circuit's **polarity**. Connect a radio with the wrong polarity and it can be damaged.
