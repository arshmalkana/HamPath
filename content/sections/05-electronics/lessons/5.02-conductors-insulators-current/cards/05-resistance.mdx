**Resistance** (symbol **R**, measured in ohms) is how much a material opposes current. An object conducts well because it has **low resistance**.

**Conductance** is simply the inverse: 1 ÷ R. High conductance = low resistance.

Don't confuse R with its look-alikes: **impedance** (Z), **reactance** (X) and **reluctance** (which is about magnetism).
