A **conductor** readily allows current to flow. Metals are conductors: **silver, copper, gold and aluminum**. Of the materials on the exam, **copper** conducts best, which is why wire is made of it.

An **insulator** blocks current: **glass, air, porcelain**, plastic, mica, dry wood, paper.

In between sit **semiconductors** like silicon, and poor conductors like carbon. Watch out for answers that slip one insulator (wood, paper, mica) into a list of conductors.
