exam_fact: In parallel, total resistance is less than the smallest resistor. N equal resistors of value R in parallel give R ÷ N.
---
Each branch is another path, so current flows more easily and the total resistance **falls**: it is always **less than the smallest** resistor.

For **equal** resistors there's a shortcut: **R ÷ N**.

- Ten resistors of value R: **R / 10**.
- Four 68 Ω resistors: 68 ÷ 4 = **17 Ω**.
- Two 100 Ω resistors: 50 Ω, which answers the hook.
