---
In series there's one path, so every resistor adds its opposition:

**R(total) = R1 + R2 + R3 + …**

- Five 10 Ω resistors: 10 + 10 + 10 + 10 + 10 = **50 Ω**.
- To replace one 120 Ω resistor: **five 24 Ω** in series (5 × 24 = 120). Six 22 Ω would give 132 Ω.

The total is always **greater than the largest** resistor.
