**Four 100 Ω resistors in parallel across a 12 V battery. What current flows?**

1. Equal resistors in parallel: 100 Ω ÷ 4 = 25 Ω.
2. I = E ÷ R = 12 V ÷ 25 Ω = **0.48 A = 480 mA**.

Or branch by branch: each 100 Ω draws 120 mA, and 4 × 120 = 480 mA.

Same method: two 1000 Ω in parallel = 500 Ω, and 12 ÷ 500 = 0.024 A = **24 mA**.
