Capacitor and inductor values are tiny, so they live at the bottom of the ladder.

- **micro (µ)** is a millionth; **pico (p)** is a millionth of a millionth.
- From pico up to micro is **two steps**, ×1000 × 1000: so 1 000 000 pF = **1 µF**.
- From micro up to milli is **one step**: 10 000 µH ÷ 1000 = **10 mH**.

Count the steps, then move the decimal point three places per step.
