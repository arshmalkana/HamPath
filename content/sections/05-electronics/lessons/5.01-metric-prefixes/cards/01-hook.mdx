title: Same frequency, three numbers
---
Your transceiver shows **7.040**. The band plan says **7040 kHz**. A net schedule lists **7 040 000 Hz**.

Are those three different frequencies, or one frequency written three ways?

Radio uses numbers from trillionths to billions. Metric prefixes keep them short. Ten minutes from now you'll switch between them without a calculator.
