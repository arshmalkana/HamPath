**Milli** is one step below the base unit: one-thousandth.

- 3000 mA on a meter marked in amperes reads **3 A**.
- A 500-milliwatt hand-held puts out **0.5 W**.
- A quarter ampere, 0.25 A, is **250 mA** (not microamperes: that would be another step down).
- Two volts is **2 000 mV**.
