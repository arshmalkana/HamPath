**An ammeter marked in amperes measures a 3000-milliampere current. What does it read?**

1. The meter's unit is amperes; the current is in milliamperes.
2. Amperes are **one step up** from milliamperes, so divide by 1000.
3. 3000 ÷ 1000 = **3 A**.

Sense check: a milliampere is small, so 3000 of them is only a few amperes.
