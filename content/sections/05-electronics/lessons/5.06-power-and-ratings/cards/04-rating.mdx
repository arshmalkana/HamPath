---
type: concept
title: The watt rating is about heat
teaches:
  - B-005-006-001
  - B-005-006-011
exam_fact: A resistor's wattage rating is set by how much heat it can safely dissipate. A physically larger resistor can dissipate more power.
---
Every watt a resistor dissipates becomes heat. Its **wattage rating** is how much heat it can get rid of safely, which is why ratings are **determined by heat dissipation**.

A bigger body has more surface to shed heat, so the large resistor is chosen **for greater power dissipation**, not for a different resistance.
