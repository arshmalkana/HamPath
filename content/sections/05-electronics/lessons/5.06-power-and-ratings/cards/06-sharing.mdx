exam_fact: Two equal 1 W resistors can dissipate 2 W in total, in series or in parallel. Two 100 Ω in parallel replace a 50 Ω resistor with twice the power rating.
---
Equal resistors share the power **equally**, whether they're in series or parallel. So two 500 Ω, 1-watt resistors can safely handle **2 W** in total either way, as you saw in the lab.

That's a useful trick: **two 100 Ω in parallel** give the **same 50 Ω** as one 50 Ω resistor, but with **twice the power rating**.
