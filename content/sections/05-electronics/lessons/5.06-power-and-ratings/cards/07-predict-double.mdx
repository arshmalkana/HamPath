---
type: predict
title: Double the voltage, then what?
teaches:
  - B-005-006-006
interaction:
  kind: predict
  prompt: The voltage across two resistors in series is doubled. What happens to the total power?
  choices:
    - text: It doubles
      feedback: "Current doubles too, and power is voltage × current: 2 × 2 = 4."
    - text: It increases four times
      correct: true
      feedback: Right. Twice the voltage drives twice the current, and P = E × I goes up 2 × 2 = 4 times.
    - text: It halves
      feedback: More voltage can't mean less power through the same resistors.
---
Then check it against the lab: 5 V gave 1.25 W, and 10 V gave 5 W.
