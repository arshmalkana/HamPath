Two of the three values give you the third:

- **P = E × I**: a 12 V load at 5 A needs **60 W**, so its transformer must be rated at least 60 watts.
- Convert milliamperes first: 12 V × 0.5 A = **6 W** of DC input power.
- **I = P ÷ E**: a 12 V, 30 W bulb draws 30 ÷ 12 = **2.5 A**.
