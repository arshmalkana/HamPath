exam_fact: Four 200 Ω, 2 W resistors in parallel make a 50 Ω dummy load good for 8 W.
---
**You need a 50 Ω dummy load that can dissipate 5 W.**

1. Four equal resistors in parallel divide R by 4: 200 Ω ÷ 4 = **50 Ω**.
2. They share the power equally: 4 × 2 W = **8 W**, more than 5 W.

Check the others: two 100 Ω in series give 200 Ω; two 25 Ω in series give 50 Ω but only 4 W; ten 500 Ω quarter-watt resistors give 50 Ω but only 2.5 W.

And two 10 Ω in series on 10 V: 20 Ω, 0.5 A, so P = 10 × 0.5 = **5 W**.
