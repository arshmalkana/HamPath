Decibels **add** where power ratios **multiply**:

| dB | Power ratio |
| --- | --- |
| 3 | × 2 |
| 6 | × 4 |
| 9 | × 8 |
| 10 | × 10 |
| 20 | × 100 |
| 30 | × 1000 |

- **+6 dB**: multiply by 4.
- 5 W → 50 W is × 10: **10 dB**.
- A 9 dB amplifier on a 2 W hand-held: 2 × 8 = **16 W**.
