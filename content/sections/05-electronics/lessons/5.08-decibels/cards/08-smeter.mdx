exam_fact: "Cutting power by 10 (−10 dB) drops a report by 10 dB; cutting it by 1000 (−30 dB) drops it by 30 dB: 100 W at S9+30 becomes S9 at 0.1 W."
---
Reports above S9 are given in dB, so power changes map straight onto them:

- 200 W → 20 W is ÷10, **−10 dB**: "S9 + 10 dB" becomes **S9**.
- 150 W → 15 W is also −10 dB: "S9 + 20 dB" becomes **S9 + 10 dB**.
- To bring "S9 + 30 dB" down to S9 you need −30 dB, ÷1000: 100 W → **0.1 W**.

Doubling to 200 W from 100 W adds only 3 dB, about half an S-unit. That's the hook's answer.
