**100 W goes into a transmission line with 6 dB of loss. How much reaches the antenna?**

1. 6 dB = 3 dB + 3 dB.
2. Each −3 dB halves the power: 100 → 50 → **25 W**.

Three quarters of your power became heat in the cable.
