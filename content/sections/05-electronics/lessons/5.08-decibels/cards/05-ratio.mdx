A **decibel** (dB) doesn't measure a single power; it measures the **ratio of two signals**:

**dB = 10 × log(P2 ÷ P1)**

The one to remember: **3 dB is a factor of 2**.

- 1 W → 2 W is **3 dB**; doubling power is always **+3 dB**.
- A **3 dB decrease** means **dividing** the power by 2.
