**Power** is the rate at which electrical energy is used. It is measured in **watts** (W).

A 100-watt bulb uses energy faster than a 60-, 50- or 40-watt bulb: the bigger the wattage, the higher the rate.

Volts, amperes and ohms measure other things: push, flow and opposition.
