To find power, **multiply voltage by current**:

**P = E × I**, so **watts = volts × amperes**.

In the lab, 12 V across 100 Ω pushed 0.12 A, so the resistor used 12 × 0.12 = 1.44 W.

Farads (capacitance) and henries (inductance) never multiply into watts.
