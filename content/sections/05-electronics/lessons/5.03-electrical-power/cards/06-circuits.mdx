exam_fact: An open circuit draws no current. A short circuit draws too much current and blows fuses. Continuity means the circuit is closed.
---
- A **closed circuit** has an unbroken path, so current flows. Testing for **continuity** means checking that the path is closed.
- An **open circuit** has a break somewhere (a broken wire, an open switch), so **no current** is drawn from the battery.
- A **short circuit** gives current an easy path that bypasses the load. With almost no resistance it **draws too much current**, which is exactly what blows a fuse the moment you switch on.
