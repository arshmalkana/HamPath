Resonance isn't razor-sharp: it matters over a range of frequencies called the **bandwidth**.

Only L and C set the resonant frequency. Bigger plates, Mylar sheets (a dielectric) or more turns on the coil all change it. **Adding a resistor** broadens the response but **leaves the resonant frequency the same**.

Receivers use resonant circuits to **select the desired signal frequencies**: the hook's answer.
