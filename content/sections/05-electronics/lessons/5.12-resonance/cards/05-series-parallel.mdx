exam_fact: At resonance a series LC circuit has low impedance (current is maximum); a parallel LC circuit has high impedance.
---
- **Series** at resonance: the reactances cancel, leaving only resistance. **Low impedance**, so **current reaches maximum**.
- **Parallel** at resonance: **high impedance**. Sweep the frequency and the impedance peaks at the **resonant frequency**.

You saw both in the labs: a dip, then a peak, at the same frequency.
