exam_fact: Resonance is when inductive and capacitive reactance are equal (and opposite in sign). A tuned circuit needs an inductor and a capacitor.
---
A coil's reactance rises with frequency; a capacitor's falls. At exactly one frequency they're **equal**. That's **resonance**.

The two reactances are **equal and opposite in sign**, so they cancel. You need both parts: an **inductor and a capacitor** form a tuned circuit.
