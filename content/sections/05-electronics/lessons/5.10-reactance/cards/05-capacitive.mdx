title: Capacitors oppose low frequencies
teaches:
  - B-005-010-002
  - B-005-010-003
exam_fact: A capacitor's reactance decreases as frequency increases (XC = 1 ÷ 2πfC), so it rises as frequency decreases.
---
A capacitor's reactance is **XC = 1 ÷ (2πfC)**: frequency is on the bottom.

So **as frequency increases, a capacitor's reactance decreases**, and **as frequency decreases, it increases**. At DC (zero frequency) a capacitor blocks completely.
