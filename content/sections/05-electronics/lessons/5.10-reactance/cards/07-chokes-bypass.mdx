Interference cures use both behaviours:

- A coil on a **ferrite core** has **high reactance at radio frequencies**, so it blocks an offending RF signal. At low frequencies its **reactance is low**, so the signals meant to flow through it hardly notice it.
- A **bypass capacitor** has **low reactance at RF**, so it diverts RF to ground, and **high reactance at audio frequencies**, so the audio circuit barely sees it.
