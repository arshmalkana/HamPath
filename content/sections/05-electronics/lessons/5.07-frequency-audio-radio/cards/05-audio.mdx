Most people can hear sound from about **20 Hz to 20 000 Hz** (20 kHz). That's why this range is called **audio frequencies**: it's what the **human ear can sense**.

Radio-frequency energy is far above it, but a receiver's job is to turn RF back into audio you can hear.
