**Wavelength** is the distance an AC signal travels during one complete cycle. Radio waves always travel at the same speed, so:

- **frequency up → wavelength down**,
- **wavelength shorter → frequency up**.

They change in simple proportion (λ = 300 ÷ f in MHz), not by squares. Watch the wavelength readout as you turn the frequency knob.
