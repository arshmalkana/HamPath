Radio spectrum is split into bands by decades of frequency:

| Band | Range |
| --- | --- |
| LF | 30 to 300 kHz |
| MF | 300 kHz to 3 MHz |
| **HF** | **3 to 30 MHz** |
| VHF | 30 to 300 MHz |

7125 kHz = 7.125 MHz, inside 3 to 30 MHz: **High Frequency (HF)**.
