One **cycle** is a full swing from positive to negative and back. **Frequency** is how many cycles happen each second, measured in **hertz** (Hz).

So **60 Hz** means **60 cycles per second**, the frequency of household power in Canada. The wall socket supplies a smooth **sine wave**.
