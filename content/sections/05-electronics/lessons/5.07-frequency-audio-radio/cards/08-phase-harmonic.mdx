Two waveforms with the **same frequency** whose cycles don't begin at the same instant are out of **phase** with each other.

A signal at an exact multiple of a fundamental frequency is a **harmonic**: 2 kHz plus 4 kHz means the 4 kHz part is the **second harmonic**.
