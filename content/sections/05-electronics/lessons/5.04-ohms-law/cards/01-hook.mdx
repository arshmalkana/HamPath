You've bought a remote antenna switch. Its relay coil is marked **25 Ω** and needs **200 mA** to pull in.

Your shack supply has three outputs: **5 V**, **8 V** and **12 V**.

Pick the wrong one and the relay either never clicks or cooks. Which output is right?

Don't guess yet. In ten minutes you'll work it out in your head.
