---
In the last lab, 0.5 A through 2 Ω needed exactly **1 V**. That's no coincidence:

**E = I × R**

- **E** is voltage, in volts (V). Radio books write E, for *electromotive force*.
- **I** is current, in amperes (A).
- **R** is resistance, in ohms (Ω).

More current, or more resistance, means you need more voltage. So you **multiply**.

Example: 0.44 A flowing through 50 Ω → 0.44 × 50 = **22 V** across the resistor.
