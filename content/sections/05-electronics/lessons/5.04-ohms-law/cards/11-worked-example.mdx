**What resistance drops 9 volts with a current of 10 milliamperes?**

1. Write down what you know: E = 9 V, I = 10 mA.
2. Convert: 10 mA = **0.010 A**.
3. Pick the formula. You want R, so R = E ÷ I.
4. Substitute: R = 9 ÷ 0.010.
5. Answer: **R = 900 Ω**.

Sense check: a small current through a sizeable voltage drop needs a big resistor. Hundreds of ohms is reasonable.
