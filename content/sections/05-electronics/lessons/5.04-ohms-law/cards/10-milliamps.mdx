Small radio circuits draw **milliamperes** (mA). One milliampere is one-thousandth of an ampere.

Ohm's Law only works when every value is in its base unit: **volts, amperes, ohms**. So convert first.

Most wrong answers to these exam questions come from skipping this step. They're off by exactly 10, 100 or 1000.
