interaction:
  kind: numeric
  prompt: "A circuit draws 300 mA from a 3 V battery. What is its resistance?"
  unit: Ω
  answer: 10
  tolerance: 0.02
  hint: "300 mA = 0.3 A. Then R = E ÷ I."
  solution: ["300 mA = 0.3 A", "R = E ÷ I", "R = 3 V ÷ 0.3 A", "R = 10 Ω"]
---
Dividing by a number smaller than 1 makes the answer **bigger**. Keep that in mind.
