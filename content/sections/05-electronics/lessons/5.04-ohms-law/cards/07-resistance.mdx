And once more, for resistance:

**R = E / I**

Think of resistance as *how many volts it takes to push each ampere*. Volts per amp means volts **divided by** amps.

From the second lab: 12 V pushing 0.25 A → 12 ÷ 0.25 = **48 Ω**.

Watch for look-alikes on the exam. **E × I** is *power*, not resistance, and Ohm's Law never squares anything.
