type: concept
title: "Current: voltage divided by resistance"
teaches: [B-005-004-002, B-005-004-008]
exam_fact: "Current equals voltage divided by resistance: I = E ÷ R."
---
Rearrange the same law to find the current:

**I = E ÷ R**

Voltage goes **on top** because more push means more current. Resistance goes **underneath** because more resistance means less current.

That's what you saw in the first lab: a 6 V battery across 30 Ω → 6 ÷ 30 = **0.2 A**.
