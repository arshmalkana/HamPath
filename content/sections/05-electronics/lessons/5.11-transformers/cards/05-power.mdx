exam_fact: In an ideal transformer power in = power out, so current changes opposite to voltage.
---
An ideal transformer doesn't make energy: **power in = power out**. So if voltage goes up, current goes **down** by the same factor, and the other way round.

- 12 V secondary from 120 V primary (÷10): 0.5 A in becomes **5 A** out.
- 1 : 5 step-up delivering 50 mA: the primary carries 5 × 50 = **250 mA**.
- 10 W at 5 V: 10 ÷ 5 = **2 A**.
- 250 W from 120 V: 250 ÷ 120 = **2.1 A** primary current.
