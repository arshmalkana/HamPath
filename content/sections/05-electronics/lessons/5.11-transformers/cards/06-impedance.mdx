type: concept
title: Changing impedance
teaches:
  - B-005-011-002
exam_fact: A transformer changes impedance because each winding carries a different voltage and current.
---
Impedance is voltage ÷ current. Each winding has its **own voltage and its own current**, so each "sees" a different impedance. That's how a transformer matches a 50 Ω radio to a very different antenna impedance.

The impedance ratio is the turns ratio **squared**.
