---
type: concept
title: Turns ratio sets the voltage
teaches:
  - B-005-011-004
exam_fact: Secondary voltage = primary voltage × (secondary turns ÷ primary turns). 250 to 500 turns doubles 120 V to 240 V.
---
A transformer's voltages follow its **turns ratio**:

**Vs = Vp × (Ns ÷ Np)**

250 primary turns and 500 secondary turns is 1 : 2, so 120 V becomes **240 V**. Twice the turns, twice the voltage.
