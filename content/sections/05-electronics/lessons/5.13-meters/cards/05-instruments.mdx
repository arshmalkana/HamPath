- A **multimeter** combines a voltmeter, ammeter and ohmmeter: **voltage, current and resistance**.
- An **RF wattmeter** connects in the feedline and **directly** reads transmitter output power.
- **Accuracy** is how close a reading is to the true value. Precision (repeatability) and resolution (smallest step shown) are different things.
