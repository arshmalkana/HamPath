An **ammeter** measures **current**, so the current has to flow **through** it: it goes **in series**, in the loop.

To avoid adding resistance it looks like a **very low resistance**. That's why connecting one across a battery is a short circuit.

Measuring a final power amplifier's current? An **ammeter**.
