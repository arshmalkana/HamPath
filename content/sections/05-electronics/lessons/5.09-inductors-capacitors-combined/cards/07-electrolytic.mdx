---
type: concept
title: Electrolytics have a + and a −
teaches:
  - B-005-009-007
exam_fact: Never apply a reverse voltage to a polarized electrolytic capacitor.
---
**Electrolytic capacitors** pack a lot of capacitance into a small can, but they're **polarized**: one lead is marked +. **Never apply reverse voltage**. A reversed electrolytic can overheat, vent or burst.

They're used for power-supply filtering and audio, not usually at radio frequencies, and they can be combined in series or parallel like any capacitor.
