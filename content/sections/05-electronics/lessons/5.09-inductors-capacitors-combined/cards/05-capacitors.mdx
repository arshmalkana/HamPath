title: Capacitors are the opposite

exam_fact: "Two equal capacitors: half the value in series, twice in parallel. Two 20 µF in series = 10 µF; two 24 µF in parallel = 48 µF."
---
Capacitance comes from plate area and spacing. Wiring capacitors **in parallel** adds plate area, so values **add**: two 24 µF in parallel = **48 µF**.

**In series** the charge has to cross two gaps, as if the plates were further apart, so the value falls: two equal capacitors give **half**. Two 20 µF in series = **10 µF**.

The rule of thumb: capacitors do the opposite of resistors and inductors.
