title: What sets L and C
teaches:

exam_fact: An air-core coil's inductance depends on its diameter, length and number of turns. An air capacitor's capacitance depends on plate area and plate spacing.
---
- **Air-core inductor**: inductance depends on the **coil diameter, coil length and number of turns**. Not the operating frequency, the wire type, or which way it points.
- **Air-spaced capacitor**: capacitance depends on the **surface area of the plates** and the **spacing between them**. Bigger plates or a smaller gap mean more capacitance. Not the voltage, frequency or type of metal.
