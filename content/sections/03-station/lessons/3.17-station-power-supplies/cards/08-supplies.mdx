- Homes get **120 V and 240 V**. A **power supply** converts **120 V AC to 12 V DC**. (An inverter does the reverse.)
- **Linear** supplies are heavy but quiet: a **lower risk of RF noise**.
- **Switching** supplies are efficient, and **smaller and lighter**, but can radiate noise.
- A simple supply with **just a transformer, rectifier and filter** has no regulator. Its voltage dips each time you key, so a CW transmitter will **chirp**.
- **Loud low-frequency hum** on your signal? Look at the **power supply** first: its filter may have failed.
