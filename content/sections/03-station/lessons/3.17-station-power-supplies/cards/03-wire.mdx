exam_fact: Heavy-gauge DC wires minimize voltage drop. The most important spec for a 13.8 V supply is its output current capability.
---
- A 100 W transceiver needs **heavy-gauge wire** to **minimize the voltage drop**.
- Count the wire **both ways**: 3 m to the battery means 6 m of wire.
- When choosing a 13.8 V supply, **output current capability** matters most. It must handle the full transmit current.
