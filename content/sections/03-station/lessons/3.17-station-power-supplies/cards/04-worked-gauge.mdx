**The radio draws 22 A, the battery is 3 m away and you may lose 0.5 V. Which is the thinnest wire you can use?**

1. Current flows there and back: 2 × 3 = 6 m of wire.
2. Allowed loss per metre: 0.5 ÷ 6 = 0.083 V/m.
3. Number 12 loses 0.11 V/m: too much. Number 10 loses **0.07 V/m**: OK.
4. Total loss with number 10: 0.07 × 6 = 0.42 V. **Number 10.**
