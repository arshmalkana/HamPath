type: concept
title: Fuse at the battery
teaches:
  - B-003-017-005
  - B-003-017-010
exam_fact: In a vehicle, fuse the positive lead as close to the battery as possible, to protect the whole circuit and prevent an overcurrent fire.
---
The fuse protects **the wire**. If it's at the radio end, a short halfway along draws huge current from the battery through unprotected wire. Put it **as near the battery as possible** to **protect the entire circuit**. The positive lead must be fused **to prevent an overcurrent from starting a fire**.
