- **Speech amplifier**: **microphones have a low output**, so this stage **amplifies the audio you want to transmit**.
- **Fixed RF oscillator**: **produces an RF carrier** at a fixed frequency.
- **Balanced modulator**: combines the audio with that carrier. It **transposes your voice from the audio spectrum to the radio spectrum**, producing two sidebands with the carrier cancelled.
