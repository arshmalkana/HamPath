- **Sideband filter**: **removes the unwanted sideband**. It works **near the fixed RF oscillator frequency**, where the sidebands are, so it never needs retuning.
- **Mixer**: combines the SSB signal with the VFO to **transpose it to the operating frequency**.
- **Variable frequency oscillator (VFO)**: your tuning dial. It **adjusts the final transmit frequency**.
