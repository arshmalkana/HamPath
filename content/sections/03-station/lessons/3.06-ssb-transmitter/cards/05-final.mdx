---
type: concept
title: The final amplifier protects itself
teaches:
  - B-003-006-008
exam_fact: In an SSB transmitter, the final amplifier normally includes protection from excessive SWR.
---
The **final amplifier** delivers the power to the antenna system. A bad mismatch sends power back into it, so it **normally includes a circuit that protects it from excessive SWR**, cutting the power back automatically.
