Three numbers tell you how good a receiver is:

- **Sensitivity**: the **RF input signal needed to achieve a given signal-to-noise ratio**. Smaller is better.
- **Selectivity**: how well it rejects signals next to the one you want.
- **Dynamic range**: how well it copes with very weak and very strong signals at the same time.
