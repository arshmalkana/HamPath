**A receiver has a 455 kHz IF and its local oscillator runs above the signal. Where must the LO be to receive 3.54 MHz?**

1. The mixer makes the IF from the difference: LO − signal = IF.
2. LO above the signal, so LO = signal + IF.
3. 455 kHz = 0.455 MHz.
4. LO = 3.54 + 0.455 = **3.995 MHz**.

Traps: adding the IF twice gives 4.450 MHz, and 13.540 MHz isn't related to the IF at all.
