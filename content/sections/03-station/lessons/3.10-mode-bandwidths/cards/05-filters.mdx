exam_fact: Use about 2.4 kHz for SSB and 250 Hz for CW with heavy interference. A CW band-pass filter around 750 to 850 Hz is ideal. Too narrow a filter loses information.
---
- **Too narrow** a filter cuts off part of the signal: **loss of information**. SSB through 250 Hz is unreadable.
- Too wide lets in extra noise and neighbouring signals.

With filters of 250 Hz, 500 Hz, 2.4 kHz and 6 kHz:

- **SSB**: **2.4 kHz**, the width of the voice signal.
- **CW with a lot of interference**: **250 Hz**, the narrowest.

CW comes out as a single tone, usually around 800 Hz, so an audio **band-pass filter from 750 to 850 Hz** passes it and little else.
