Narrowest to widest:

1. **CW**: about 150 Hz.
2. **SSB voice**: about 2.4 kHz.
3. **FM voice**: about 12 kHz or more.

A **waterfall** (spectrogram) shows **frequency across** the screen and **amplitude as brightness**, scrolling down as time passes.
