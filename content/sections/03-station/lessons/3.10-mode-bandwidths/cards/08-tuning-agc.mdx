title: Tuning accuracy and AGC
teaches:

  - B-003-010-005
exam_fact: SSB must be tuned accurately, within about 100 Hz. AGC limits volume changes caused by large changes in signal strength.
---
- **SSB** has no carrier, so the receiver's BFO must sit in exactly the right place. More than about **100 Hz** off and voices sound high or low pitched. AM and FM carry their own carrier and are forgiving; CW mistuning just changes the tone's pitch.
- **Automatic gain control (AGC)** turns the receiver's gain down for strong signals and up for weak ones. It **limits the change in volume** when signal strength varies a lot.
