---
Big filter capacitors can hold a lethal charge long after the supply is unplugged. Once the cabinet is open, **discharge the filter capacitors first**, using an **insulated shorting stick with a resistor in line**. Don't trust bleeder resistors (they can fail), and don't use a screwdriver.
