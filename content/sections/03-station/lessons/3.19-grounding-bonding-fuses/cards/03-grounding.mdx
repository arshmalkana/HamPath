---
- **Ground all station equipment**, with every chassis connected to a **single-point ground**.
- **Bond all ground electrodes together** with heavy conductors. That **prevents voltage differences** between devices if lightning strikes.
- The best ground rod is **copper-clad steel**: strong to drive, good contact, resists corrosion.
- In a three-wire line cord, the **green wire goes to the chassis**. If a fault touches the chassis, the current goes to ground and the **chassis can't become energized**.
