---
type: concept
title: When the ground wire is too long
teaches:
  - B-003-019-007
exam_fact: A long ground wire from an upper-floor shack can have high impedance at the operating frequency, giving RF burns.
---
At radio frequencies a long wire isn't a short circuit. A 10 m ground lead from a third-floor shack can be a quarter-wave or so on HF: **high impedance at your operating frequency**. The equipment isn't at RF ground, and you can get an **RF burn** touching it.
