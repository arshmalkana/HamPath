- **SWR meter**: shows whether the **antenna system's impedance is matched** to the transmitter. (An S-meter shows received signal strength; a multimeter measures DC and low-frequency quantities.)
- **Antenna tuner**: matches the antenna system to the radio. It can let you use an antenna on a **band it wasn't designed for**. A solid-state final amplifier needs a good match, so the tuner may need **adjusting whenever you change frequency**.
- **Antenna switch**: selects **which antenna, or the dummy load**, the station is connected to. It doesn't tune or turn anything.
- **Dummy load**: a big, shielded resistor that **absorbs the RF as heat** and **doesn't radiate**. Use it for testing.
