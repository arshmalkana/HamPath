A transmitter makes small amounts of energy at **harmonics**: two, three, four times the operating frequency. A **low-pass filter** passes the operating frequency and blocks those harmonics, so its job is to **reduce harmonic emissions**. Modern transmitters have one built in.

Where an external one goes:

- No external amplifier: **as close as possible to the transceiver output**.
- With an external amplifier: **as close as possible to the amplifier's output**, after the last thing that could create harmonics.

The **SWR meter** comes **right after the low-pass filter**, then the tuner, then the antenna switch.
