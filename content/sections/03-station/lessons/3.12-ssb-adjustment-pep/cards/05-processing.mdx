---
A speech processor lifts the quiet parts of your voice toward the peaks. A little makes you easier to understand. **Too much** causes **audio distortion and splatter** onto stations **near your frequency**.

The same goes for the amplifier: an SSB **power amplifier must be linear**. A non-linear one mangles the voice until it's **unintelligible**.
