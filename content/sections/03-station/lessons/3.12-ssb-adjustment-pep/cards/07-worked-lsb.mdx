exam_fact: LSB on 7100 kHz with a 1000 Hz tone transmits on 7099 kHz.

**Your transmitter is on lower sideband at 7100 kHz. You modulate it with a single 1000 Hz tone. Where does the RF come out?**

1. The carrier at 7100 kHz is suppressed.
2. Lower sideband sits **below** the carrier, offset by the audio frequency.
3. 1000 Hz = 1 kHz, so 7100 − 1 = **7099 kHz**.

Upper sideband would give 7101 kHz.
