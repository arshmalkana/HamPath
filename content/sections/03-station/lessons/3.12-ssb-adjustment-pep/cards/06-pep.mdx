- **Peak envelope power (PEP)**: the **average power during one RF cycle at the crest of the modulation envelope**. It's how SSB power is measured.
- An amateur **SSB** signal is **between 2 and 3 kHz** wide.
- The **balanced modulator** **suppresses the carrier and passes both sidebands**; the filter after it removes one.
- Even in double sideband, **suppressing the carrier** lets **more of the output power go into the sidebands**, where the information is.
