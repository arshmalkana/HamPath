- **ALC (automatic level control)** **limits the audio peaks** so the transmitter **isn't overdriven**.
- Set the **mic gain** so the **ALC meter never goes past its maximum range** on voice peaks. Not full deflection.
- **Too much gain** overmodulates: the signal is **distorted and takes up more bandwidth**, so it **interferes with stations near your frequency**. Not on other bands: the splatter sits right beside you.
