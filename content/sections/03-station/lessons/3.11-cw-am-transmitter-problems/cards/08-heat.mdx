type: concept
title: Where the rest of the power goes
teaches:
  - B-003-011-010
  - B-003-011-011
exam_fact: The difference between DC input power and RF output power of an amplifier appears as heat.
---
An amplifier with **200 W** of DC input and **125 W** of RF output is wasting 75 W. That power doesn't vanish: it's **dissipated as heat** in the amplifier, which is why finals have heat sinks and fans. The difference between DC input and RF output always **appears as heat**.
