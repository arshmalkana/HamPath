**Amplitude modulation (AM)** changes the **amplitude** of the RF wave to carry information. Its **envelope**, the outline of the waveform, rises and falls with the audio.

An AM signal contains the **carrier and two sidebands**, one each side of the carrier. Each sideband is as wide as the highest audio frequency, so voice up to **3 kHz** gives an AM signal **6 kHz** wide.

FM, phase modulation and FSK change the frequency or phase instead.
