- **Chirp** is a small change in the output **frequency each time a dit or dah is sent**. The usual cause: the supply voltage dips when the transmitter keys, and the oscillator's frequency moves with it. Cure it by **keeping the power supply voltages very steady under varying loads**.
- A slow change as the oscillator warms up is **drift**, not chirp. That's why an RF oscillator must be **electrically and mechanically stable**: so it **doesn't drift in frequency over time**.
- A **VFO** in a CW transmitter means your frequency **isn't limited to the crystals you own**.
