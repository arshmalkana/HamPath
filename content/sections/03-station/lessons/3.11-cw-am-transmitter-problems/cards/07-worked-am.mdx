**Your voice goes up to 3 kHz. How wide is your AM signal?**

1. AM makes two sidebands, one above and one below the carrier.
2. Each sideband is as wide as the audio: 3 kHz.
3. Total: 2 × 3 kHz = **6 kHz**.
