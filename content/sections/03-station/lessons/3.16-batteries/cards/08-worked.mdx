**You have two 12 V, 20 Ah batteries. What do you get in parallel, and in series?**

1. Parallel: same voltage, capacities add. 12 V, 20 + 20 = **40 Ah**.
2. Series: voltages add, same capacity. 12 + 12 = **24 V**, 20 Ah.
