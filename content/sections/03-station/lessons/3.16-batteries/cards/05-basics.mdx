- A battery is a **source of electromotive force (EMF)**, with a **positive and a negative terminal**. A lithium-ion cell is one; diodes, FETs and resistors aren't sources.
- A car **starter battery** supplies about **12 volts**.
- A battery you can recharge again and again is a **storage battery**. A primary battery is single-use.
- **NiMH** cells are preferred over alkaline because they **can be recharged** many times.
