---
type: concept
title: Series adds volts, parallel adds ampere-hours
teaches:
  - B-003-016-009
  - B-003-016-010
exam_fact: "Two 12 V, 20 Ah batteries: in parallel 12 V, 40 Ah; in series 24 V, 20 Ah."
---
- **Parallel**: the voltage stays the same and the capacities add. Two 12 V, 20 Ah batteries make **12 V, 40 Ah**.
- **Series**: the voltages add and the capacity stays the same. Two make **24 V, 20 Ah**.
