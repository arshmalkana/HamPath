- Every battery has a little **internal resistance**. Draw current and some voltage is lost inside it, so the **terminal voltage drops**.
- Capacity is rated at a certain current. Draw **more** and the battery **discharges faster than specified**: you get fewer ampere-hours.
- **Lithium** batteries pack **much more capacity per kilogram** than lead-acid: ideal for portable operation.
- A **lithium-ion battery must never be short-circuited**: it can overheat and catch fire.
