---
type: concept
title: If someone is in contact with high voltage
teaches:
  - B-003-018-008
  - B-003-018-009
exam_fact: Turn off the power first, never touch the person while the source is live. Then call for help and give first aid.
---
1. **Turn off the power** first. **Never touch** the person while they're still in contact: you'd become part of the circuit.
2. **Call for emergency help**.
3. **Give first aid** if needed.
