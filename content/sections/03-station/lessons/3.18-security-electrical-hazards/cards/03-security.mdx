title: Keep unauthorized users off the air
teaches:
  - B-003-018-001
  - B-003-018-002
exam_fact: At home, use a key-operated switch in the main power line. In a car, remove the microphone when you're not using it.
---
You're responsible for every transmission from your station.

- At **home**, a **key-operated on/off switch** in the main power line stops anyone using it.
- In a **car**, **take the microphone with you**. Turning the radio off isn't enough: anyone can turn it back on.
