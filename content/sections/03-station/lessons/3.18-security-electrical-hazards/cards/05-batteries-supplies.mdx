- A **car battery** won't shock you at 12 V, but it can deliver a **huge short-circuit current**: enough to weld a wrench and start a fire.
- **Interlock switches** cut a high-voltage supply when its cabinet opens, so **no one gets shocked**.
- Before tracing a fault in a mains supply, **turn it off and unplug it**. Working on a **live** supply risks **electric shock**.
