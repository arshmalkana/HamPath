1. **Microphone**: turns **air pressure changes** into an **electrical signal** that drives the **speech amplifier**. It's audio, not RF.
2. **Speech amplifier**: makes that weak audio stronger.
3. **Modulator**: in FM it **changes the oscillator's frequency** in step with the audio. That's what makes FM different from AM, CW and SSB, where the oscillator's frequency stays put.
4. **Oscillator**: makes the RF signal, now swinging in frequency.
5. **Frequency multiplier**: produces a **useful harmonic**, multiplying the oscillator's frequency up to the **FM output carrier frequency**.
6. **Power amplifier**: brings it up to full power.
