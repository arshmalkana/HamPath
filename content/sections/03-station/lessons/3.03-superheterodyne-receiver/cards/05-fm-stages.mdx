FM receivers add two stages after the IF:

- **Limiter**: clips the signal to a constant level, **removing amplitude variations**: noise and fading. FM carries information in frequency, so nothing is lost.
- **Discriminator**: turns frequency swings back into audio, **recovering the original modulation**.

Then the **AF amplifier** drives the speaker. It's the stage the **volume control** adjusts, and in an FM receiver it includes the **squelch**, which mutes the hiss when no signal is present.
