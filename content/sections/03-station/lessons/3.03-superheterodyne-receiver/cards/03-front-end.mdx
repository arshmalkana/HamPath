- **RF amplifier**: the **front end**, the first stage after the antenna. On VHF any noise it adds gets amplified by everything after it, so it must be designed to produce **very little noise**.
- **Mixer**: combines the signal with the local oscillator to make a fixed **intermediate frequency (IF)**. That's what lets the later stages and the detector work at a **single frequency, whatever you tune to**.
- **Local oscillator**: tuning the dial changes it, so the local oscillator **sets the received frequency**.
