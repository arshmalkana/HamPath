- When you're not operating, **ground all antenna and rotator cables** to help protect the station and the building.
- A **lightning surge protector** goes **outside**, **close to the earth ground**, so the surge never enters the house.
- The **best protection** of all: **disconnect the equipment** from both the power lines and the antenna cables.
