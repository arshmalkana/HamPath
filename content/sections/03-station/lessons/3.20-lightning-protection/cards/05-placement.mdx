exam_fact: Mount wire antennas high enough that no one can touch them from the ground, because touching an antenna can cause RF burns. Keep people away from open-wire line and from ground-mounted antennas.
---
- A horizontal wire antenna goes **high enough that no one can touch any part of it from the ground**. Touching an antenna while you transmit can cause **RF burns**.
- **Open-wire feedline** carries high RF voltages: anyone touching it could be **burned**.
- A **ground-mounted** vertical needs **people kept at a safe distance**.
