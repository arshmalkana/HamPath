- **Boom**: the tube everything is mounted on. It's **mechanical support**, not a radiating element.
- **Driven element**: a half-wave dipole in the middle, **connected to the transmission line**.
- **Reflector**: behind it, the **longest** element. It sends energy forward.
- **Director**: in front, the **shortest** element. It pulls energy forward.

The signal goes out toward the director.
