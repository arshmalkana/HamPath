---
The interface provides **audio-frequency coupling** between the computer and the transceiver. Not RF: the radio does all the RF work.

- **Receive**: converts the transceiver's **analog audio into a digital signal** for the computer.
- **Transmit**: converts the computer's **digital signal into audio** the transceiver can transmit.
- **PTT**: most interfaces also **switch the transceiver between receive and transmit**.

Decoding the characters is the computer software's job; displaying the frequency is the radio's.
