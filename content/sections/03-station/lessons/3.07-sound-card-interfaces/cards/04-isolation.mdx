title: Isolation transformers and built-in codecs
teaches:

exam_fact: Isolation transformers keep the computer-radio connection from adding hum and interference. Radios with a built-in audio codec don't need a separate sound card.
---
- Connecting a computer and a radio can create ground loops that put **hum and interference into your transmitted signal**. **Isolation transformers** in the interface break that path.
- Some transceivers have the whole interface inside: they **incorporate an audio codec**, the chip that turns audio into data and back. Plug in a USB cable and the computer sees a sound card. (USB alone, DSP or CAT control wouldn't do that.)
