title: What the key does
teaches:

exam_fact: "The key switches the carrier on and off: it controls the carrier's amplitude."
---
Morse is the carrier **switched on and off**. Press the key and the carrier appears; let go and it disappears. So the key **controls the amplitude of the carrier**: full or nothing.

It doesn't switch between two frequencies, it doesn't key an audio tone, and it doesn't break the antenna connection. In a three-stage transmitter it usually keys the driver or amplifier, leaving the oscillator running steadily.
