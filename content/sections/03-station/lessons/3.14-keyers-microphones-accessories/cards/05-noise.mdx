- A **noise blanker** recognizes **high-amplitude, short-duration pulses**, like ignition noise or electric fences, and removes them. It's most effective on **impulse noise**, not continuous hiss.
- **DSP noise reduction** **analyzes the noise and signal characteristics** and partially removes the noise, even steady background hiss.
