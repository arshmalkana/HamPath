---
- New microphone? First adjust the **microphone gain**.
- A **dynamic** microphone has a coil and magnet, built **like a small loudspeaker**.
- **VOX** (voice-operated transmit) switches the transmitter on automatically **when you speak**. (A VXO, VCO and VFO are oscillators.)
- A properly adjusted **speech processor** **improves intelligibility**. With the transmitter already at 100% modulation, it **raises the average power**; the peak power can't go any higher.
