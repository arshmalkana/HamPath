- **Deviation** is the **change in frequency caused by modulation**.
- A **higher level of modulating signal** gives **a larger deviation**, not a bigger amplitude.
- Set to 2.5 kHz on a 5 kHz repeater, **your audio will be low**.
- **Speaking too loudly** over-deviates: reports say you're **loud and distorted**, yet on frequency.
