An FM signal is roughly twice the deviation plus twice the highest audio frequency: with **5 kHz** deviation and 3 kHz audio, about 16 kHz, so **between 10 and 20 kHz**.

- That's too wide for the crowded HF bands: below 28 MHz the **bandwidth would exceed the limits in the regulations**.
- FM is closely related to **phase modulation**: both change the carrier's phase angle.
- On VHF and UHF, FM gives a **good signal-to-noise ratio at low signal levels**, which is why it's the standard for local contacts.
