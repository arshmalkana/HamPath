---
- With no signal, an FM receiver's **very large gain ahead of the discriminator** amplifies plain noise into a loud rush. Squelch mutes it.
- When two signals share a channel, the FM receiver tends to hear **only the strongest**: the **capture effect**.
- If your **microphone fails**, the transmitter still sends a carrier, just with no modulation: **an unmodulated carrier**.
