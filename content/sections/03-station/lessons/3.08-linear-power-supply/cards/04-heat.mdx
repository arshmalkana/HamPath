A linear regulator holds the voltage steady by burning off the difference as heat. At full current that's a lot of heat, so the **voltage regulator needs a heat sink**.

If the regulator fails, it can pass the full unregulated voltage straight to your radio. **Overvoltage protection** watches **the regulator's output** and shuts the supply down if the voltage rises too high.
