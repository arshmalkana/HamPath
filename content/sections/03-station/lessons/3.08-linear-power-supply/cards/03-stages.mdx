1. **Transformer**: **steps the AC mains voltage down (or up)** and **isolates** the output from the mains.
2. **Rectifier**: diodes **convert AC into DC**, pulsating DC at first.
3. **Filter**: large capacitors **smooth out the pulsating DC**.
4. **Regulator**: **keeps the voltage constant** however much current the radio draws.
