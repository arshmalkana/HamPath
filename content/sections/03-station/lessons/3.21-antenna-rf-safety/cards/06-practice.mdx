- At **UHF and microwave**, keep antennas **away from your eyes** whenever RF is applied.
- Hold a **handheld's antenna away from your head and away from others** while transmitting, to reduce exposure.
- Put a UHF transmitting antenna **where no one can get near it** while you transmit.
- Mount **high-gain beams higher than nearby structures**, so they don't point RF into neighbours.
- A **dipole's ends and centre** go **as high as possible**, out of reach.
- **Indoor antennas** go **as far as possible from rooms people will be in** while you operate.
- Before removing a **UHF amplifier's shielding**, make sure it **can't be turned on accidentally**.
