- **Antenna**: **converts electromagnetic waves into electrical currents**.
- **RF amplifier**: boosts weak signals, **increasing the receiver's sensitivity**.
- **Mixer**: converts the received signal **into the intermediate frequency**.
- **Local oscillator**: its signal **is mixed with the incoming signal to produce the IF**.
