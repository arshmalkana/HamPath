- **Beat frequency oscillator (BFO)**: makes the missing carrier. Its signal **is mixed with the IF** to **recover the transmitted modulation**.
- **Product detector**: where that mixing happens. It **recovers the modulation**: speech for SSB, a tone for CW.
- **AF amplifier**: **increases the level of the recovered modulation** to drive the speaker. An **audio band-pass filter** can go here too, for example a narrow one centred on your CW tone.
