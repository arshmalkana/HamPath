exam_fact: In an SSB/CW receiver the IF filter provides most of the selectivity and the IF amplifier provides most of the gain.
---
- **IF filter**: provides **most of the selectivity**: it decides how close another signal can be before you hear it.
- **IF amplifier**: provides **most of the receiver's gain**.
