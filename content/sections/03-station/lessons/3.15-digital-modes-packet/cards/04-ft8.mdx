- **FT8** is about 50 Hz wide, so **dozens fit in the space of one SSB signal**. It also decodes signals far below the noise: it works at the **lowest signal-to-noise ratio** of the common modes.
- **RTTY** is about 250 Hz wide. To avoid interference, set up **250 to 500 Hz** away (centre to centre) from a contact in progress.
