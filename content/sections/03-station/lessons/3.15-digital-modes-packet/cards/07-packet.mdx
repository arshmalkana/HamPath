- A **digipeater** **receives digital data and retransmits the data marked for retransmission**.
- A **network** connects packet stations so **data can travel long distances**, hop by hop.
- Packet's **reliable messaging**, guaranteed delivery or a failure notice, makes it useful for **emergency communications**.
- **ARQ** (automatic repeat request) resends any packet that arrives damaged: that's **error correction**, not just error detection.
