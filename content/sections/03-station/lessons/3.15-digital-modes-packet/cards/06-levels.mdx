---
With sound-card modes, **too much audio** overdrives the transmitter and causes **splatter**: out-of-channel emissions over nearby signals. The ALC should be idle, not limiting.

How to check: **ask a local station to confirm your signal is free of splatter**. Just being decoded proves nothing: a splattering signal decodes too.
