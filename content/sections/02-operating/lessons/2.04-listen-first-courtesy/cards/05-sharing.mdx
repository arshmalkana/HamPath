- **Net control** and the usual frequency is busy? **Ask the occupants** if they'd move; otherwise **move the net** to a clear frequency.
- A net about to begin on **your** frequency? As a courtesy, **move**.
- Interference growing as propagation changes? **Move**: no one owns a frequency.
- Leave about **3 kHz** from an SSB contact in progress.
- A **band plan** is a **voluntary division** of a band that keeps **incompatible modes** apart.
