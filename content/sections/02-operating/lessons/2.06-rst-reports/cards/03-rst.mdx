An **RST** report describes **how you're being received**: **R**eadability, **S**ignal strength, **T**one.

| Digit | Range | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R | 1 to 5 | 1 unreadable, 3 considerable difficulty, 4 quite readable, 5 perfectly readable |
| S | 1 to 9 | 1 barely perceptible, 3 weak, 5 fair, 7 moderately strong, 9 strong |
| T | 1 to 9 (Morse only) | 9 perfect tone |

- "**5 9 plus 20 dB**": perfectly readable and 20 dB **stronger than S9**.
- Reporting on a station heard **through a repeater**? Report **its signal and audio quality as heard through the repeater**.
