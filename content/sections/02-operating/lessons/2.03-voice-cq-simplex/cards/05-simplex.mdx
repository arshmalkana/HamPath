**Simplex** is transmitting and receiving on the **same frequency**: no repeater.

- Use it **when signals are reliable** between you without a repeater. To check, **listen on the repeater's input** frequency: if you can hear the other station there, you can go simplex.
- Choose simplex frequencies carefully, so you **don't land on a local repeater's input**. If you find yourself on a repeater frequency, **move**: you may be **interfering with the repeater's users**.
- Keep **local** contacts on **VHF and UHF**, leaving HF **clear for long-distance** contacts.
