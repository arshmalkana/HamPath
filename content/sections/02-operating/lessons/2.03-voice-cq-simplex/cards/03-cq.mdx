- **Calling CQ**: "CQ" **three times**, then "**this is**", then your call sign **three times**.
- **Answering a CQ**: the other station's call sign **once**, "**this is**", your call sign.
- **Calling a specific station**: their call sign, "**this is**", your call sign.
