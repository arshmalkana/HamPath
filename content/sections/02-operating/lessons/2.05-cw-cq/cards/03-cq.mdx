---
- **CQ in Morse**: "CQ" **three times**, "**DE**", your call sign **three times**.
- **Answering**: their call sign **twice**, "**DE**", your call sign **twice**.
- Send at **a speed you can reliably receive**: whoever answers will reply at your speed.
