- Stranded but **without immediate risk to life**? Your message is **urgent**, not distress.
- If a station in **distress breaks in** on your contact, **acknowledge it** and find out **where it is and what help it needs**.
- If you **can't help directly**, **contact the authorities** and **keep listening** in case you're needed.
