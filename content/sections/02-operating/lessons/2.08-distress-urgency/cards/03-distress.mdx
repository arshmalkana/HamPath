- Transmit **SOS** or **MAYDAY** only in a **life-threatening distress situation**.
- **Voice**: "**MAYDAY**" three times. **Morse**: **SOS**.
- On a **repeater**, **break in between transmissions** with your **call sign and situation**.
- **Distress** traffic comes before everything, including an **emergency** (urgent) message.
