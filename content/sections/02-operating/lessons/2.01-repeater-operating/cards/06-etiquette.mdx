- To call a station you know: say **their call sign, then identify with yours**. No "breaker, breaker", no calling three times.
- To **join a conversation**, say **your call sign during a break** between transmissions.
- **Pause** before and between transmissions so **anyone else wanting to use the repeater** can get in.
- Plain language: ask "**Where are you?**", not "What's your 10-20?".
