- A **CTCSS** tone is a **sub-audible tone** sent with your voice. The repeater only opens its **audio output when the tone is present**, so it ignores other signals.
- The **time-out timer** **interrupts lengthy transmissions**.
- Keep transmissions short: a long one **may stop someone with an emergency** from getting through.
