A **repeater** **increases the range of portable and mobile stations**. It listens on one frequency (its **input**) and retransmits on another (its **output**). On **2 m** the standard **offset** between them is **600 kHz**.

**Frequency coordination** recommends repeater frequencies carefully so **neighbouring repeaters don't interfere** with each other.
