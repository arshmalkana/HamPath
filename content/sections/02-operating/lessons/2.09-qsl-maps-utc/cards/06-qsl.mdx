- A **QSL card** is **written proof of a contact** between two amateurs. Today, **QSL cards or web-based logging systems** both count as proof.
- Asked for your **grid square** (common in VHF contests)? It's your **location as a 4- or 6-character code**, like FN25dk.
- Keeping a **logbook** isn't a **regulatory requirement**, though it's useful for awards, memories and interference complaints.
