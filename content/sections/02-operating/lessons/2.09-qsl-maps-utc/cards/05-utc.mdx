- **UTC** is measured from the meridian at **Greenwich, United Kingdom**.
- Local time = UTC + your offset. At **UTC − 6**, a **19:00 UTC** net is at 13:00: **1 PM** local.
- **CHU** (Canada), **WWV** and **WWVH** broadcast **accurate time and frequency signals**.
