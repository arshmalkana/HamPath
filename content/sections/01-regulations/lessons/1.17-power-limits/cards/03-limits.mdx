| | Basic (any) | Advanced |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DC input to the final stage | **250 W** | **1000 W** |
| SSB peak envelope power | **560 W** | **2250 W** |
| Carrier power, other emissions | **190 W** | 750 W |
| 60 m (ERP, PEP) | **100 W** | **100 W** |

Honours or Morse code don't change Basic power limits.
