**You hold Basic and Morse code. What's the most SSB peak envelope power you may use on 3750 kHz?**

1. Morse code doesn't change power limits; only Advanced does.
2. The Basic limit for SSB is **560 W PEP**.
3. Answer: **560 W**, though you should use only what you need.
