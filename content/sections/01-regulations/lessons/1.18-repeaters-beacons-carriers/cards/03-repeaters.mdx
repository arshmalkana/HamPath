- A **repeater** station **automatically retransmits** the signals of other stations.
- With only the Basic qualification, the **lowest repeater output** you may use is **29.500 MHz**: the **29.500 to 29.700 MHz** segment of 10 m. 10 m repeaters typically use a 100 kHz offset.
