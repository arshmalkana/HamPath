**You want to call CQ on 7.250 MHz. Is that inside the 40 m band?**

1. 40 m runs from **7.0 to 7.3 MHz**.
2. 7.250 is above 7.0 and below 7.3.
3. **Yes**, with 50 kHz to spare below the top edge.
