- Whoever's station it is, you use **only the privileges of your own qualifications**: as control operator elsewhere, or when someone more qualified uses your station.
- To operate **below 30 MHz**, you need **80% on the Basic exam** (Honours), or the **Advanced** or **Morse code** qualification.
- **Radio-controlled models**: allowed on **all amateur bands above 30 MHz**.
