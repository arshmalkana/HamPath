- Transmitting **outside the amateur bands** is **prohibited**, and penalties can fall on the **control operator**.
- A fake "MAYDAY" is a **false or fraudulent message**. **False information is never permitted**, not as a joke and not to exaggerate an emergency.
- **False distress** messages, and **interfering with radio communication** without lawful cause, carry **a fine up to $5000, a prison term up to one year, or both**.
- The **Radiocommunication Act** sets out these offences and penalties.
