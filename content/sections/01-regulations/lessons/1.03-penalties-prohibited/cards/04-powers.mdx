---
- The Minister may **suspend** a certificate if the holder broke the Act or regulations, got it by **misrepresentation**, or **didn't pay** fees, but **not without notice** and a chance to make representations.
- A **radio inspector** may **not** enter a **dwelling** without the occupant's consent or a **warrant**. With a warrant, force is allowed only with a **peace officer** and when authorized. The occupant must provide the information requested.
