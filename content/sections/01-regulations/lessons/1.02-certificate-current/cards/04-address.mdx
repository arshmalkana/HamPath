- **Moved?** Tell **ISED within 30 days** of a mailing address change, even within the same province. You can keep operating meanwhile.
- A **first certificate** is **free**.
- Moved to a new province or territory? The **replacement call sign** with the new prefix is **free**.
- **Changing** an existing call sign (including to a two-letter call) costs **$60**.
