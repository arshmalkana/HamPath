---
- **Both** the **control operator** and the **station owner** are responsible for how a station is operated. Operate someone else's station and you're **both** responsible.
- The owner is responsible for **operating according to the regulations**, and may choose **any qualified operator** as control operator.
- A station needs a control operator **whenever it's transmitting**, and they must be at the **control point**.
