- **Health Canada** publishes **Safety Code 6**, which gives **RF exposure limits for the human body**.
- Limits are **lowest from 48 to 300 MHz**, where **the human body absorbs RF energy the most**.
- Besides tissue heating, the other established effect, especially below 10 MHz, is **nerve stimulation**.
- SC6 sets limits on **field strengths**, **induced and contact currents**, and **specific absorption rate**, not on the power fed to antennas.
