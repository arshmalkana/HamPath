- SC6 covers **controlled and uncontrolled environments**. Limits for the general public (uncontrolled) are **lower**.
- **No transmitters are exempt**: handhelds, broadcast and low-power transmitters too.
- It doesn't set a "safe power" for a handheld: that's **not specified**. Exposure depends on gain, distance, power and frequency. Electric and magnetic field limits use different units.
