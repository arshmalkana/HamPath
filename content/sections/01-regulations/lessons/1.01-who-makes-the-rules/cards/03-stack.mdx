- The **Radiocommunication Act** is the top. It gives the authority to **make regulations** and to **publish the Standards** for amateur stations.
- **Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED)** **administers** the Act.
- The **Radiocommunication Regulations** **define** the "amateur radio service".
- The **Standards for the Operation of Radio Stations in the Amateur Radio Service** (RBR-4) set the day-to-day operating rules.

The Broadcasting Act covers broadcasters, not amateurs; the ITU Radio Regulations are international.
