- **No age limit**, young or old.
- You must pass the **Basic Qualification** exam and have **a valid address in Canada**. Citizenship isn't required.
- Holders of the **Radiocommunication Operator General Certificate Maritime (RGMC)** may be issued an amateur certificate.
- After Basic, add the **Advanced** and **Morse code** qualifications in **any order**. Morse code needs **5 words per minute**.
