---
- **Business communications are not permitted under any circumstance**. A club net can plan recreation, code practice or emergencies, not **business**.
- You may **never broadcast** to the general public.
- One-way transmissions that **are** allowed: model control, brief adjustments, Morse practice.
- **Q codes** are fine. Rebroadcasting a broadcaster's programming, business traffic or commercially recorded material is not.
