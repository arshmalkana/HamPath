- Messages to foreign amateurs must relate to **the purposes of amateur radio** or be **remarks of a personal nature**.
- Canadian amateurs also follow the **International Telecommunication Union's (ITU) Radio Regulations**.
- Under the ITU rules, international contacts are **permitted unless an administration has registered an objection**.
- Each **administration decides** whether **Morse code proficiency** is required.
