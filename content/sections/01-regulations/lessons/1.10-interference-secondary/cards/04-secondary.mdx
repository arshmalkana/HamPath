- On a band where amateurs are **secondary**, you may use it **only if you don't interfere with primary users**, and you must accept their interference.
- **430 to 450 MHz**: amateur operation must **not interfere** with other services there.
- **902 to 928 MHz**: amateurs are **not protected** from other services, and it's crowded with **licence-exempt devices**.
- Between two amateurs, **both have an equal right** to a frequency, whatever their qualifications or power.
