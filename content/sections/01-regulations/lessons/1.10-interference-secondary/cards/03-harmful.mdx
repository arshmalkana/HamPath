- A transmission that **disturbs**, **seriously degrades, obstructs or repeatedly interrupts** other communications is **harmful interference**.
- **Deliberate interference is never acceptable**, even against a station operating illegally.
- If the Minister finds your station causes harmful interference, they can **order its operation to stop or change**.
- **Test** transmissions are fine only when they **won't interfere** with any station, amateur or otherwise.
