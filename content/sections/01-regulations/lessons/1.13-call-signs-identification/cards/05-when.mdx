- Identify **at the beginning and end** of every contact, and **at least every 30 minutes** in between.
- **Each station gives its own call sign**, at the start and at the end.
- The rules are **the same for tests** as for contacts.
- The **only** unidentified transmissions allowed: **controlling a model craft**.
- Identify in **English or French**.
