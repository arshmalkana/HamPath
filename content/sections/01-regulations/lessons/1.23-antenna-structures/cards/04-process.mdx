- You may not need consultation when an **ISED exclusion criterion** applies, or the **municipal process** excludes the structure (**either one**). The tallest you can build without consultation is **the tallest those exclusions allow**.
- **No local process?** Follow **ISED's default process**: written notice, then address **reasonable and relevant concerns** raised **in writing within the 30-day** comment period. Public meetings aren't part of it.
- **Local process exists?** The **municipality** decides how consultation takes place.
