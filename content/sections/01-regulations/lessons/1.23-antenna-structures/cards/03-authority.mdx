- The **Minister of Innovation, Science and Industry** has authority over antenna masts and towers, not the municipality or the neighbours.
- You must follow **ISED's antenna siting procedures** and address community concerns responsibly. You **can't** erect any size of structure without consulting.
- If you and a stakeholder can't agree, **ISED makes the final decision**.
