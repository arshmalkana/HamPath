- In the **United States**, including **7 km off Florida** (US waters), you follow **US** band limits.
- **Basic** is enough to operate in the US. On voice: your **Canadian call**, "**portable**" or "**mobile**", then the **US call area** (VE3HAM portable W4).
- A **CEPT** licence, for Europe, needs **Advanced**. Identify with **the visited country's prefix**, "**stroke**", then your call (F stroke VE3HAM).
