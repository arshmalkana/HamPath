- A disabled candidate may be examined **orally** or in a **tailored** way; a Morse sending test may be **recited as Morse sounds**. They are **not exempted** from portions of the exam.
- A candidate who fails a written exam because of **reading skills** or not usually speaking English or French may get an **oral** exam. An **interpreter** isn't allowed. An examiner may ask for **medical evidence** before accommodating.
