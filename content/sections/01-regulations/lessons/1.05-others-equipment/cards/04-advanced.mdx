type: concept
title: Other radio apparatus
teaches:
  - B-001-005-001
exam_fact: An Advanced operator may install, modify or repair radio apparatus for another person only if that person holds an authorization for it.
---
With the **Advanced Qualification**, you may install, place in operation, modify or repair **radio apparatus** for someone else **if they hold an authorization for that apparatus**. Not while it's "pending".
