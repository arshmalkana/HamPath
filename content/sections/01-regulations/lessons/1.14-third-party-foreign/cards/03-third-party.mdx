- **Third-party** communications are on behalf of someone without a certificate. They must be **personal and non-commercial**.
- Canada **doesn't prohibit** international third-party traffic, so you may pass it **with any amateur station**.
- You can't contact a country that has **filed an objection with the ITU**.
- Emergency and disaster third-party traffic is allowed **unless the foreign administration prohibits it**.
- With non-certified friends at both ends, **both of them** are third parties. If a foreign station joins your friend's conversation, **keep monitoring**.
