- Visitors may operate if their **country has an agreement with Canada** and they've **obtained the permit**.
- **CEPT** licence holders get **Advanced** privileges.
- **Americans** in Canada: give **location by city and province** at least once per contact, and on voice add "**mobile**" or "**portable**" and the **Canadian prefix** for where they are (for example, K1ABC portable VE3).
