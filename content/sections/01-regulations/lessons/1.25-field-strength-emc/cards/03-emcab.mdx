ISED's **Electromagnetic Compatibility Advisory Bulletin EMCAB-2** compares the **field strength of your emissions on your neighbour's premises** with ISED's **immunity criteria**:

- **Below** the criteria: the equipment's **lack of immunity** is the cause.
- **Above** the criteria: **your transmission** is the cause.

Whether you're within your power limit, or it stops when you stop, doesn't decide it.
