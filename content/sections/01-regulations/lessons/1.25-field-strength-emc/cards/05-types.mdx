EMCAB-2 sets criteria by equipment type:

- **Associated equipment**: devices often in home entertainment systems that **aren't strictly radio apparatus**, like amplifiers and speakers.
- **Radio-sensitive equipment**: devices that use radio to work, like a **garage door opener**.
