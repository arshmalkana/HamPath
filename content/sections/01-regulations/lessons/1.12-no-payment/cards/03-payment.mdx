- **No payment of any kind**, not money, gifts, equipment or repairs, for passing messages.
- Transmissions **by amateur stations** may be divulged or used.
- Other **non-broadcast** radiocommunications you overhear must stay private. Exceptions include **protecting property or preventing harm**, **court evidence**, and **national defence or security**. Passing them to the **media** is an **offence**.
