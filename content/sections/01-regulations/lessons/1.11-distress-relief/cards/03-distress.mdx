- An operator **in distress** may use **any means of radiocommunication**, with **no power limits**.
- If a distress call goes **unanswered** on a band you have no privileges for, **you may respond** and offer help.
- The only time interfering with another station is allowed: when **you're directly involved in a distress** situation.
