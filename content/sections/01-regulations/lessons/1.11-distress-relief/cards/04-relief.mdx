- Amateurs communicate **only with other amateurs**, and using frequencies **outside the amateur bands is never permitted**, even during relief.
- You may transmit to support relief when **normal communications are overloaded, damaged or disrupted**.
- Not in the net? **Avoid needless transmissions** on or near its frequency.
- Messages from **public service agencies** can be handled in **peacetime, civil emergencies and exercises**.
- One thing you may **broadcast**: communications needed for the **safety of life and property**.
