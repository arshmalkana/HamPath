**May you use FM, about 15 kHz wide, on 29.600 MHz?**

1. 29.600 MHz is in 10 m (28.000 to 29.700 MHz).
2. The 10 m bandwidth limit is **20 kHz**.
3. 15 kHz is under 20 kHz: **yes**. On 20 m, with its 6 kHz limit, it wouldn't be.
