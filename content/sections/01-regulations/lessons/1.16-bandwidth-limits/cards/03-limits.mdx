| Band | Maximum bandwidth |
| --- | --- |
| 160 m to 12 m (except 30 m) | **6 kHz** |
| 30 m (10.100 to 10.150 MHz) | **1 kHz**: no SSB |
| 10 m (28 to 29.7 MHz) | **20 kHz** |
| 6 m and 2 m | **30 kHz** |
| 70 cm and up | wide enough for fast-scan TV |
