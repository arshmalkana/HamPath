- Near a **band edge**, make sure your **entire occupied bandwidth** stays inside the band.
- **Fast-scan TV** (about 6 MHz wide) is **prohibited on 145 MHz** and on HF like **14.23 MHz**; it's fine on **440 MHz**.
- **SSB** is prohibited on **30 m** (10.12 MHz). CW and FT8 are fine there.
