- Amateur stations talk to **stations operated under similar authorizations**: other amateurs. Not military, contest-only or any station heard on the band.
- **Never** use amateur equipment to transmit **outside the amateur bands**; it isn't certified for that.
- A dual-band radio may be used on **land mobile** frequencies only if it's **certified and licensed for the land mobile service**.
- You may not operate amateur apparatus on aeronautical, marine or land mobile frequencies.
