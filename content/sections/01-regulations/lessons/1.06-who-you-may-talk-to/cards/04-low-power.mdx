title: Even low power needs supervision

exam_fact: A 2 W amateur station must still be under the supervision of a certificate holder. Amplifying a licence-exempt transmitter is never permitted.
---
- Even a **2-watt** amateur station is under regulatory control: it must be supervised by **a certificate holder with a call sign**.
- Using an amplifier on a **licence-exempt** transmitter, like FRS or Wi-Fi gear, is **never permitted**.
