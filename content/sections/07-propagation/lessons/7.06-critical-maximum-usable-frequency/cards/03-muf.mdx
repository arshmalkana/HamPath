- **Critical frequency**: the highest frequency a region can reflect when probed **straight up** (vertical incidence).
- **Maximum usable frequency (MUF)**: the **highest frequency that will reach a given destination**.
- Signals that hit the ionosphere at an **oblique** angle come back at frequencies well **above the critical frequency**: that's how two continents can talk.
- Below the MUF, signals are **bent back to Earth**.
- The MUF varies with the **amount of solar radiation**, and **rises each day** as UV increases.
