- **Skip** happens because the ionosphere **refracts** the signal back down some distance away.
- The **skip distance** is the **minimum** distance reached after one trip to the ionosphere.
- The **skip zone** lies between the **end of the ground wave** and where the **first sky wave returns**: too far for one, too close for the other.

Geometry decides the distance:

- **Higher radiation angle**: shorter skip. The **lowest angle** gives the greatest skip.
- **Higher refracting region**: longer skip.
