- One hop off the **F2** region reaches up to about **4000 km**.
- One hop off the **E** region, lower down, reaches about **2000 km**.
- So **5000 km** to Europe needs **multi-hop**: the signal comes down, is **reflected** by the Earth's surface, and goes back up.
