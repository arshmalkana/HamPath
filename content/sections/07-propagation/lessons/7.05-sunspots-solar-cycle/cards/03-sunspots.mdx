- **More sunspots, more ionization**.
- The **solar cycle** averages **11 years**, and it's the main cause of **cyclical changes** in HF propagation.
- At high sunspot numbers, frequencies **up to 40 MHz or higher** carry long-distance contacts. Not 100 MHz.
