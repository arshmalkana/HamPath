- **Solar radiation** influences **all** communication beyond ground wave and line of sight, and **the sun** affects **every frequency** to some degree.
- Day to day, the sun's **electromagnetic and particle radiation** shapes the ionosphere.
- How well the ionosphere refracts HF depends on **the amount of solar radiation**, not your power or receiver.
