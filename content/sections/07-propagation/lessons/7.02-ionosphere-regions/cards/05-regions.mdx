---
From the bottom up:

- **D region**: the **lowest**, there only in daylight. It **absorbs** the low bands, so **160 m and 80 m** are short-range by day. It's the **least useful** for long distance.
- **E region**: **below the F region**.
- **F region**: in daytime it **splits into F1 and F2**. **F2** is the **highest**, so it returns signals from furthest away.
