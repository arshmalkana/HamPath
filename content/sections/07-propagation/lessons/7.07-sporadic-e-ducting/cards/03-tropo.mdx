The lower atmosphere bends VHF signals gently back toward the ground. That **tropospheric bending** **lets you reach stations farther away**. The part of the wave kept close to the Earth this way is the **tropospheric wave**.

Without enhanced conditions, normal VHF tropospheric propagation reaches about **800 km** at best.
