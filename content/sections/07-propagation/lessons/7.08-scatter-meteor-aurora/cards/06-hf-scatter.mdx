- **Scatter** lets **weak signals** be heard in the **skip zone**: too far for ground wave, too close for sky wave.
- They're **weak** because **only a small part of the energy** is scattered there.
- They sound **distorted** because the energy arrives **through several paths**.
- **Weak, distorted signals close to the MUF** usually mean scatter.
