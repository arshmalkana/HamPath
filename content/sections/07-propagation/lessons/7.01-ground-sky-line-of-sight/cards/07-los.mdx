exam_fact: Nearby VHF stations communicate by line of sight. Normal refraction in the troposphere stretches the VHF radio horizon beyond the visible horizon.
---
VHF and UHF signals normally pass straight through the ionosphere, so two nearby VHF stations talk by **line of sight**. The lower atmosphere bends the signal slightly toward the ground, **normal tropospheric refraction**, so the radio horizon is a little beyond the visible one.
