A **sky wave** (also called the **ionospheric wave**) goes **up to the ionosphere and is returned to Earth**. That's how HF signals reach **beyond 4000 km**.

Beam the signal almost straight up and it comes down all around you: **NVIS**, near vertical incidence sky wave. It gives **medium-range HF coverage**, filling in valleys and **difficult terrain** that ground wave can't.
