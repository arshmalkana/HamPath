In daylight the **D region absorbs** the lower HF frequencies. That's why distant **160 m and AM broadcast** stations disappear in the daytime: the **D region is ionized**.

A **sudden ionospheric disturbance** (SID), from a solar flare, boosts D absorption suddenly. Absorption is worst at low frequencies, so **try a higher band**.
