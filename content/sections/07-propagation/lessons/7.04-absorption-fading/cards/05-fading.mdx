When a signal arrives by **two or more paths**, the copies arrive out of phase:

- They add and cancel: **fading**. Small changes in the ionosphere make the strength go up and down.
- They also cause **phase distortion**.
- **Selective fading** happens when different frequencies within one signal fade differently, because of **phase differences between its components**. It's **worse on wide signals**.
- With a 2 m handheld in the city, moving **less than a metre** can take you from a spot where reflections add to one where they **cancel**.
