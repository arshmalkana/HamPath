exam_fact: Adding series inductance lowers an antenna's resonant frequency. A trap is a coil and capacitor in parallel.
---
- Adding **series inductance** (a loading coil) makes an antenna act electrically longer: it **decreases the resonant frequency**.
- A **trap** is a **coil and capacitor in parallel**. At its resonant frequency it blocks current, cutting the antenna off at that point; that lets one antenna work on several bands.
