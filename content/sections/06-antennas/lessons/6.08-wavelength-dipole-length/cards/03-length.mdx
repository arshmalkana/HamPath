exam_fact: To lower an antenna's resonant frequency, lengthen it; to raise it, shorten it. End insulators limit the antenna's electrical length.
---
- **Longer** antenna, **lower** resonant frequency. To move a dipole from 3900 kHz down to 3600 kHz, **make it longer**.
- **Shorter** antenna, **higher** resonant frequency. From 3600 to 3900 kHz, **make it shorter**.
- **Insulators** at the ends of a wire antenna **limit its electrical length** to the wire between them.
