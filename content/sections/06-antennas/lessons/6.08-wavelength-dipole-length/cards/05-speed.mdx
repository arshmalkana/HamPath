Radio waves travel at **the speed of light**: **300 000 kilometres per second** in free space.

Wavelength in metres = **300 ÷ frequency in MHz**:

- 25 MHz: 300 ÷ 25 = **12 m**.
- 2 MHz: 300 ÷ 2 = **150 m**.
