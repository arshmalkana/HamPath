---
- Length in metres ≈ **143 ÷ f (MHz)**: 28.150 MHz gives **5.08 m**; 3.75 MHz gives **38.13 m**.
- Feed-point impedance in free space: about **73 Ω**.
- Its 3D pattern is a **torus (doughnut)** around the wire.
