- A **random wire** antenna is fed right at the station, so **RF feedback** into your equipment is a common problem.
- An **end-fed half-wave (EFHW)** can work **several bands** with one wire, through a matching transformer.
- A **trap** antenna works on **several bands**, but it **may radiate harmonics more readily**, since it's resonant on several frequencies.
