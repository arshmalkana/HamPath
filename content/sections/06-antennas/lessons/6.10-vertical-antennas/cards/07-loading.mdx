- A whip much shorter than a quarter-wave is **capacitive**. An **inductor in series**, the **loading coil**, **tunes out the capacitive reactance** and makes it resonate.
- On VHF and UHF mobile, a **5/8-wave** vertical has **more gain** than a quarter-wave: it squeezes more energy toward the horizon.
