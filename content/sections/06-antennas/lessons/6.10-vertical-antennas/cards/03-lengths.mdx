- Quarter-wave length in metres ≈ **71.3 ÷ f (MHz)** below 30 MHz. For 21.125 MHz: 71.3 ÷ 21.125 = **3.37 m**.
- On VHF, a **half-wave** is about 150 ÷ f: for 223 MHz, about **67 cm**.
- A quarter-wave vertical on a car roof radiates **equally in all horizontal directions**.
