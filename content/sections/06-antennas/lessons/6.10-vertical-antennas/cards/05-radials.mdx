- With horizontal radials an elevated quarter-wave is about 36 Ω. **Sloping the quarter-wave radials downward** brings it **close to 50 Ω**, a good match for **50 Ω coax**.
- A **ground-mounted** vertical needs a **wire ground system (radials)** at its base to reduce ground losses.
