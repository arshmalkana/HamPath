**Polarization** is the orientation of the radio wave's **electric field relative to the Earth's surface**:

- **Horizontal**: electric field **parallel** to the Earth's surface.
- **Vertical**: electric field **perpendicular** to it.

The antenna sets it: a Yagi with **horizontal elements** is **horizontally** polarized; a half-wave antenna **perpendicular to the ground**, or a dipole **mounted vertically**, is **vertically** polarized.
