---
- A wave that leaves **vertical** and travels by **ground wave** arrives **vertical**.
- To receive a mobile station's **vertical whip**, use a **vertical** antenna, such as a ground plane.
- A vertical antenna receives a vertically polarized wave at **higher strength** than a horizontal one. Cross-polarized, you can lose 20 dB or more.
