- **Front-to-back ratio**: power radiated **forward** divided by power radiated in the **opposite direction**.
- **Thicker elements** give **wider bandwidth**.
- **Element traps** let one Yagi work **20, 15 and 10 m**.
- **Stacking** two identical antennas doubles the power aperture: **+3 dB**. Two 10 dBi Yagis stacked: **13 dBi**.
