- **Longer boom, more directors**: **gain increases**.
- **Wider element spacing**: **higher gain**.
- For a 3-element Yagi, about **0.20 λ** spacing is the **best compromise** between gain and front-to-back ratio.
- Yagis are popular from **20 m to 10 m** because the elements are **short enough** to make a **rotatable** high-gain antenna.
