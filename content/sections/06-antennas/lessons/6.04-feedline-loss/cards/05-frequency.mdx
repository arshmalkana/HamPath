exam_fact: Line loss increases with frequency because of internal line losses. RG-58 on 70 cm has excessive loss. Switching from RG-213 to RG-58 means less power radiated.
---
- Loss **increases with frequency**, due to **internal losses** in the conductors and dielectric.
- That's why thin **RG-58** on the **70 cm** band means **excessive loss**.
- Swap **RG-213** for **RG-58** with the same transmitter and **less power is radiated**.
