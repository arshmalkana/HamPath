---
- Line loss is quoted in **decibels per unit length**, such as dB per 100 m.
- So loss **increases with length**. Going from **20 m to 40 m doubles** the loss in dB: an increase of **100%**.
- Got 60 m of coax for a 15 m run? **Shorten the excess cable**. Coiling it up doesn't help.
