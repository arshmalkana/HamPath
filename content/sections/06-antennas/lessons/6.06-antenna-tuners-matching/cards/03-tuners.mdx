- A modern solid-state transceiver **automatically reduces power** when it sees an **excessive mismatch** with its transmission line.
- An **antenna tuner matches** the transceiver to a **mismatched antenna system**. It does it by **adding capacitive or inductive reactance**.
- That lets the transceiver **deliver its rated power**. It doesn't reduce loss in the line beyond the tuner.
