exam_fact: Maximum power transfers when load equals source impedance. Matching at the line-antenna junction minimizes line losses; a tuner at the feed point gives less line loss. If the antenna is matched to the line, line length doesn't matter.
---
- **Maximum power** goes to a load when the **load impedance equals the source impedance**.
- To minimize **line loss**, the match is needed **where the line meets the antenna**. A tuner at the antenna feed point means **less transmission line loss** than one in the shack.
- If the antenna is correctly matched to the line, the **line's length has no effect** on the match.
