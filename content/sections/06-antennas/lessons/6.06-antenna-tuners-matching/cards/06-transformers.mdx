exam_fact: A transformer can match over a wide bandwidth. An EFHW's high feed impedance is matched to 50 Ω coax with a transformer. Matching 300 Ω to 50 Ω takes a 6:1 ratio.
---
- A **transformer** can be designed to match **over a wide bandwidth**, with no retuning.
- An **end-fed half-wave** has a **very high** feed impedance; a **transformer** matches it to 50 Ω coax.
- The ratio is just the two impedances divided: **300 ÷ 50 = 6**, so **6:1**.
