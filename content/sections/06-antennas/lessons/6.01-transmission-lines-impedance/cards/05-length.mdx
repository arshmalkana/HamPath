---
type: concept
title: Length doesn't change Z0
teaches:
  - B-006-001-003
exam_fact: Cutting a 52 Ω line shorter leaves its characteristic impedance at 52 Ω.
---
A 20 m length of 52 Ω line, cut down to 10 m, is still **52 Ω**. Characteristic impedance is a property of the cross-section, the same at every point along the line.
