A **transmission line** connects your transceiver to your antenna. Its **characteristic impedance** (Z0) depends on the **physical dimensions and relative positions of the conductors**, not on length, frequency or what's connected at the end.

- **Coax**: Z0 depends on the **ratio of the shield's diameter to the inner conductor's**. That's why thick and thin cables can both be 50 Ω: the **ratio** is the same.
- The **velocity factor**, how fast the wave travels compared with free space, depends mostly on the **dielectric material**.
