---
- Terminate **50 Ω coax** in a **50 Ω** resistive load and the load **absorbs all the energy**: nothing reflects.
- Terminate it in a very **different** impedance and the impedance you measure at the input becomes **a value that depends on the line's length**.
- **Coaxial cable** can be **buried directly** in the ground for some distance; open-wire, window line and twin-lead can't.
