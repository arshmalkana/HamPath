A **parasitic element** isn't connected to anything. It's **energized by induction or radiation from the driven element**, and re-radiates. Adding parasitic elements **increases a dipole's directivity**.

- Add a **slightly shorter** element: radiation increases **from the dipole towards the new element** (a director).
- Add a **slightly longer** one: radiation increases **from the new element towards the dipole** (a reflector).
