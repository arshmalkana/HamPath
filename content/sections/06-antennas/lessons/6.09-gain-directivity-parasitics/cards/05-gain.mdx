**Antenna gain** is the ratio of an antenna's radiated signal strength to that of a **reference antenna**.

- **dBi**: compared with the **isotropic** radiator.
- **dBd**: compared with a half-wave dipole.
- A **half-wave dipole** has about **2.1 dBi** of gain in free space.

So **dBd = dBi − 2.1**: 4.1 dBi is **2.0 dB** over a dipole.
