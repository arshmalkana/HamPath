teaches:

exam_fact: An open or short circuit causes high SWR; an intermittent connection causes erratic readings. High SWR's main effect is increased line loss.
---
- An **open or short circuit** in the antenna system gives a **high SWR**.
- **Erratic** readings usually mean an **intermittent connection** somewhere.
- The main harm of operating at high SWR is **increased transmission line loss**.
