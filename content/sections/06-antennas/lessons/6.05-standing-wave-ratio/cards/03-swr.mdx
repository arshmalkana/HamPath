exam_fact: SWR 1:1 is the best match; under 1.5:1 is fairly good. A mismatch between line and antenna produces standing waves, reducing energy transferred to the antenna. The SWR meter measures forward and reflected voltage.
---
- If the line's characteristic impedance doesn't match the antenna, **standing waves** form on the line and **less RF energy reaches the antenna**.
- An **SWR meter** measures **forward and reflected voltage** and works out the ratio.
- **1:1** is the **best match**. Below **1.5:1** is a **fairly good** match.
