---
type: concept
title: Loss makes SWR look better
teaches:
  - B-006-005-011
exam_fact: Line loss decreases the SWR reading at the station because the reflected energy is attenuated.
---
Reflected power loses energy travelling back down a lossy line. So **line loss makes the SWR at the station look lower** than it really is at the antenna. A suspiciously good SWR on a long, lossy run can hide a bad antenna.
