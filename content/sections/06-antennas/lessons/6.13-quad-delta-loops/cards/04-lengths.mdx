Full-wave loop length in metres ≈ **306 ÷ f (MHz)**:

| Frequency | Loop length |
| --- | --- |
| 21.4 MHz | **14.30 m** |
| 14.3 MHz | **21.40 m** |
| 28.7 MHz | **10.66 m** |
| 7.15 MHz | **42.80 m** |

A loop is a little longer than 300 ÷ f, the free-space wavelength.
