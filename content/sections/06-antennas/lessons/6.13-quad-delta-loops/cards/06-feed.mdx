- Feed a delta loop in the **centre of the horizontal bottom** element for **horizontal** polarization.
- Move a quad's feed point from a horizontal side to a **vertical side** and the polarization changes **from horizontal to vertical**.
- A quad's wire and spreaders are **more susceptible to weather damage** than a Yagi's aluminium tubes.
