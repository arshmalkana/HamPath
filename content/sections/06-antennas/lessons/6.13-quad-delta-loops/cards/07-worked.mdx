**How much wire for a horizontal loop on 7.15 MHz?**

1. Full-wave loop (m) ≈ 306 ÷ f (MHz).
2. 306 ÷ 7.15 = **42.80 m**.

Using 300 ÷ f gives 41.96 m, the free-space wavelength: a loop needs a little more.
