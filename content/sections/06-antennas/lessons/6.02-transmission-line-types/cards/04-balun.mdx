A line is **unbalanced** when **one conductor is connected to ground**, like coax's shield.

A **balun** (BALanced to UNbalanced) **connects balanced and unbalanced systems**:

- Feeding a **dipole** (balanced) with **coax**: the balun goes **between the coax and the antenna**.
- Joining **coax to window line**: use a balun.
- A tuner with **no balanced output** feeding **window line**: put a balun between them.
