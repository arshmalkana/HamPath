- **Coaxial cable**: a centre wire inside insulating material, covered by a metal **shield**. It's **unbalanced**: one conductor (the shield) is grounded.
- **Window line**: two wires **side by side, embedded in insulation** with windows cut out.
- **Open-wire line**: two conductors held a constant distance apart by **insulated rods (spreaders)**.

A **balanced** line is **two parallel wires** carrying equal and opposite currents.
