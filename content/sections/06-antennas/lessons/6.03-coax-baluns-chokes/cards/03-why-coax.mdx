The field of a coaxial line stays **inside the shield**, so nearby metal, ice and walls don't affect it. That's why coax is used most: it's **usable in a wide variety of settings**. Other lines can have less loss.

Shielding, best to worst: **solid shield**, double braid, single braid, foil.
