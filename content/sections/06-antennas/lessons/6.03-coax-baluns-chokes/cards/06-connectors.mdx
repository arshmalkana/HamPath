| Connector | Where you see it |
| --- | --- |
| **PL-259** (UHF) | On RG-213, for an HF transceiver |
| **SMA** | Handheld transceiver to its antenna |
| **N** | Designed to be **moisture resistant** |
| **BNC** | Low-power transceivers and test instruments |

**Clean and tighten** connectors regularly to keep **contact resistance** low.
